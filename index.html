The problem

A missed call is usually a missed job.

When someone has water on the floor, they don't leave a voicemail. They hang up and call the next company on Google.

By the time you're off the roof or out from under the house, the job is booked with someone else. Often you never find out what it was.

How it works

Four steps. Nothing new to learn.

StepWhat happens
  1. 1
    Your phone still rings first.

    If you can pick up, you answer like always. Nothing changes.

  2. 2
    Missed calls forward to the assistant.

    If you don't answer after a few rings, your phone company sends the call over. It's one setting, and I set it up with you.

  3. 3
    It takes the details in English or Spanish.

    Name, callback number, address, the problem and the best time to call. It reads the number and address back to the caller.

  4. 4
    You get the summary by email within a minute.

    Call the customer back when you're free, with everything in front of you.

Ground rules

Rules it follows on every call.

It answers with your company's name, so these are fixed. They are not settings anyone can switch off.

Posted rules · every assistant I set up
  • Always says it's an AI.

    It tells every caller at the start. It never pretends to be a person.

  • Never quotes repair prices.

    Prices come from you, after you've talked to the customer.

  • Never promises an arrival time.

    It tells the caller someone will call back. You decide when you can go.

  • Gas smell or flooding: safety and 911 first.

    It tells the caller how to get safe and to call 911 before anything else. Then it takes the details and marks the call URGENT.

  • Never calls anyone on its own.

    It only answers incoming calls. No cold calls, no robocalls in your name.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is it a robot?

Yes. It's an AI voice assistant, and it tells every caller that at the start of the call. It sounds natural, but it never pretends to be a person.

What if it gets something wrong?

It reads the callback number and address back to the caller before hanging up, so most mistakes get caught on the call. You see every detail in the email. If something is off, tell me and I change what it asks or how it confirms.

What happens in an emergency?

If a caller mentions a gas smell, flooding, sparks or smoke, it tells them how to get safe and to call 911 first. Then it takes the details and marks your email URGENT. It doesn't send anyone out. That stays your call.

Can I turn it off?

Anytime. Turn off call forwarding on your phone and missed calls go back to voicemail like before. It takes a few seconds.

Does it work with my current number?

Yes. You keep your number. Your phone rings first like always, and only the calls you don't pick up go to the assistant.

Does it speak Spanish with my customers?

Yes. It answers in English and switches to Spanish as soon as the caller speaks Spanish. Your summary email comes in whichever language you prefer.
